Disclaimer Notice to Whomsoever it May Concern

We bring to the notice of all concerned that we, that is, JSW Group Companies, have deployed a merit-based employee selection practice. We do not charge or accept any amount or security deposit from job seekers during the selection process or while inviting candidates for an interview.

We have noticed that fake job offers in the name of companies like JSW Steel Limited, JSW Coated Products Limited, JSW Energy Limited, JSW Cement Limited and certain other JSW companies, as well as fictitious entities, have been circulated by some unauthorised persons/fraudsters. Some fraudsters are using the names of JSW companies to solicit job applications and require job seekers/applicants to pay processing fees or deposit amounts by sending false emails or by making fraudulent telephone calls.

If any person receives any unsolicited or fraudulent communication offering a job or an interview call from a JSW company against payment of money, it is advised not to respond. On receipt of an interview call for any job in any JSW Group company, candidates are advised to visit the official website at www.jsw.in and submit their query through the contact form available on the website to verify the authenticity of the communication and obtain relevant information.

We shall not accept any liability towards any representation made in fraudulent communications or their consequences, and such communications shall not be treated as any kind of offer or representation by any JSW company.

Visit the Careers section of our website for authentic openings across various JSW Group companies.
